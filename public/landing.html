Why a streak, and not just a to-do list?

A to-do list only tells you what's left. It doesn't tell you what you've built. A streak is the opposite — it's proof, compounding daily, that you're someone who shows up.

That's the whole bet behind StudySync: gamification isn't decoration bolted onto productivity, it's what makes the boring, consistent stuff feel worth doing.
